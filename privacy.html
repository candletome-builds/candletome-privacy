Privacy Policy

Effective November 25, 2026

Who operates the app

CandleTome Reader is operated by Nariman Bekbulatov. For privacy questions or requests, email bek.ar.nariman@gmail.com.

Books and reading data

The app stores imported books, reading progress, bookmarks, highlights, notes, preferences, and layout cache locally. We do not operate a server that receives your books or reading activity. Device motion used to orient the reader is processed on the device. The app does not include advertising, advertising tracking, or an analytics SDK.

If you import a file from iCloud Drive or another file provider, that provider handles the source file under its own policy. CandleTome Reader does not provide its own cross-device synchronization for books or notes. Copies may exist in your source folders or device backups, depending on your settings.

Purchases

In versions that offer paid import, Apple processes your payment through your Apple Account. The app uses Apple’s StoreKit to check and restore access. We do not receive your payment card details.

Support and website visits

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you send. We use it to respond and troubleshoot and keep it as needed to handle your request or comply with applicable obligations. These pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information such as IP addresses when serving the site. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Your choices and rights

You can manage locally stored information through the app and your device settings. Contact us to ask about information you shared with support. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete that information and to complain to your data protection authority. We will update this page when our practices change.
